# Pocketey Japan — Draft Image Generation Policy

Create ONE editorial hero image for the supplied Pocketey Japan article draft.

The image is part of a human-reviewed travel-news draft. It must support the article without inventing evidence.

## Core policy

- The image must be directly relevant to the article topic, location and traveler context.
- Prefer a calm, premium editorial look consistent with Pocketey Japan: restrained, modern-classic, quiet, useful and trustworthy.
- Use a wide 16:9 composition suitable for an article hero and card crop.
- Do not place headlines, captions, fares, dates, warning labels, route names, station signs or other generated text inside the image.
- Do not reproduce logos, trademarks, official emblems, operator branding or copyrighted artwork.
- Do not depict an identifiable real person or imply that a generated person is a real traveler, official, employee or witness.
- Do not fabricate documentary evidence. A generated image must never look like proof that a specific disruption, accident, disaster, closure, crowd condition or weather impact actually occurred.
- For weather, earthquake, volcano, safety or disruption stories, use a restrained illustrative/atmospheric visual. Do not invent damage, flooding, stranded passengers, emergency scenes, ash coverage, cancellations or dangerous conditions that are not established in the draft.
- For transport stories, show generic infrastructure or travel atmosphere rather than fabricated timetables, ticket screens, station notices or operator-specific details.
- For rules, fares, tickets and procedures, use a conceptual travel scene or object-based editorial composition rather than fake documents or UI screenshots.
- For events, do not invent specific costumes, performers, decorations or crowd scenes unless they are actually supported by the article.
- Avoid sensationalism, exaggerated weather, dramatic disaster imagery, clickbait visual language, fantasy elements and tourist stereotypes.
- No embedded text unless the source material absolutely requires it; by default use no text at all.

## Visual direction

Pocketey Japan visual language:
- sophisticated travel-editorial photography or refined editorial illustration
- natural light or soft atmospheric light
- warm ivory, charcoal, muted olive/gray and natural Japan-inspired colors
- uncluttered composition with useful negative space
- believable scale and geography without pretending to reproduce a specific incident
- no game-like, cartoonish or glossy advertising aesthetic

The output will be labeled on Pocketey as an illustrative image when AI-generated.
