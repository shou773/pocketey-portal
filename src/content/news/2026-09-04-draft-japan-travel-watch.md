September is a transition month for travel in Japan. Transport timetables, seasonal events and booking systems can change, so a quick check of official information before a long-distance journey can save time at the station or on the road.

JNTO's current travel-brochure listings include an updated bus schedule for Kawaguchiko and Mt. Fuji effective from September 1, 2026. Travelers heading toward the Fuji Five Lakes area should check the latest timetable rather than relying on an older itinerary or screenshot.

## September events are now in season

JNTO has also published its September 2026 events calendar. If your trip is flexible, it is worth checking the official calendar before locking in day trips: local festivals and seasonal events can add a great experience, but they can also affect accommodation availability and local transport demand.

## Shinkansen users should watch scheduled booking-system maintenance

JR Central's travel booking information lists several overnight maintenance windows in September during which reservations, cancellations and some QR-ticket functions may be unavailable. If you are traveling around one of those windows, save or prepare the information you need before maintenance begins and re-check the official service notice close to departure.
