As of September 7, 2026, JR Central has integrated real-time operational data for the Tokaido Shinkansen into the 'Japan Travel by NAVITIME' mobile application. This update allows international travelers to receive live status information, including service delays and operational changes, directly within the navigation platform.

## What travelers need to know
Previously, travelers often had to check separate websites or station boards to confirm the status of the Tokaido Shinkansen. By integrating this data into the NAVITIME app, JR Central aims to provide a more seamless experience for those navigating Japan’s high-speed rail network. The integration covers the entire Tokaido Shinkansen line, which connects major hubs including Tokyo, Nagoya, Kyoto, and Osaka.

## What you should do
If you are planning to travel on the Tokaido Shinkansen, ensure you have the latest version of the 'Japan Travel by NAVITIME' app installed on your smartphone. Check the app for real-time updates before heading to the station, especially during periods of inclement weather or heavy travel seasons when service disruptions are more likely.

## What is confirmed so far
JR Central has confirmed that the real-time data feed is now active within the 'Japan Travel by NAVITIME' application as of September 7, 2026. No changes to ticketing or boarding procedures have been announced in conjunction with this digital update.
