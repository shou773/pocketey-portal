The program is a pilot project, meaning it is currently being tested to evaluate demand and operational feasibility. While the service is designed to facilitate cycling with pets, travelers should be aware that this is a new offering and specific equipment availability may be limited during the initial phase.

If you are planning to visit Hamana Lake with a pet and wish to utilize this service, check the official JR Central news release for specific booking requirements, weight limits for pets, and rental procedures.
