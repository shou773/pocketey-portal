## The essential information

Event coverage will put dates, location and major access details near the top rather than burying them under a long destination essay.

## Why it may matter for your itinerary

Popular events can change crowd levels, hotel demand and transport conditions. Pocketey will highlight those knock-on effects when they are useful.
