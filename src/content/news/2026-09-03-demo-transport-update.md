## What changed?

For real transportation updates, this section will summarize the announcement in plain English without forcing readers to decode a long operator notice.

## Who is affected?

Pocketey will focus on the practical traveler impact: dates, stations, lines, ticket types, reservation requirements and whether visitors need to change an existing plan.

## What should travelers do?

The final section will give the simplest next step and link to the relevant railway operator or other primary source whenever possible.
