## What travelers need to know

A Level 5 Flood Emergency Warning signifies that a disaster is already occurring or is imminent. Local authorities may issue mandatory evacuation orders for areas along the river. Travelers in Aichi and Gifu prefectures should be aware that this situation may lead to sudden, localized transport disruptions, including road closures and potential service suspensions for trains and buses operating near the river basin.

- **Avoid the area:** Do not approach the Shonai River or low-lying areas near the riverbank.
- **Follow local instructions:** If you are currently in an affected area, prioritize your safety and follow all evacuation orders or guidance provided by local municipal authorities or hotel staff.
