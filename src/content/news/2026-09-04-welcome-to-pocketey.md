## What Pocketey is for

Japan is easy to love and surprisingly easy to over-research. Useful information is often scattered across railway notices, government pages, tourism boards, local announcements and news reports.

Pocketey Japan is designed to make that stream easier to use. The goal is not to publish everything. It is to identify changes that may alter a traveler's plan and explain them in clear English.

## What we will cover

Expect practical updates about transportation, seasonal events, disruptions, new visitor rules and other changes that can affect a trip.

Each update aims to answer three questions:

1. **What changed?**
2. **Why does it matter to an international traveler?**
3. **What should you do next?**

## A deliberately small beginning

Pocketey is starting as a compact independent publication. The design and publishing system are intentionally lightweight so that attention can stay on accuracy, usefulness and learning what readers actually need.
