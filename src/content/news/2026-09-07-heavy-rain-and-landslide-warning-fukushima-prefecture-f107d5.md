The Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA) has issued formal heavy rain warnings for the Hamadori region of Fukushima Prefecture. Authorities are urging strict vigilance regarding the risk of landslides and flooding in low-lying areas.

## What travelers need to know

As of September 7, 2026, the JMA has placed the Hamadori region under a heavy rain warning, specifically citing risks of landslides and inundation. In addition to the heavy rain, thunder, strong winds, and high waves are forecast for the area. 

Travelers in or planning to visit Fukushima Prefecture, particularly the Hamadori coastal area, should be aware that severe weather can lead to localized flooding and potential disruptions to regional transportation networks.

## What you should do

*   **Monitor local updates:** Keep a close watch on the [JMA website](https://www.jma.go.jp/) or local news for the latest weather developments.
*   **Follow local guidance:** Adhere to any instructions or evacuation guidance provided by local municipal authorities.
*   **Check transport status:** If you have travel plans involving trains, buses, or flights in the Fukushima area, check the official websites of your transport operators for real-time information on delays or service suspensions.
*   **Exercise caution:** Avoid areas prone to landslides or flooding, such as riverbanks and steep slopes, during periods of heavy rainfall.

## What is confirmed so far

*   **Formal Warnings:** The JMA has issued an 'Oame Keihou' (Heavy Rain Warning) for the Hamadori region, with specific concerns for landslides and flooding.
*   **Additional Advisories:** Thunder, strong wind, and high wave advisories are also in effect for the Hamadori region.
*   **Regional Scope:** While the most severe warnings are concentrated in the Hamadori region, various parts of Fukushima Prefecture, including the Nakadori and Aizu regions, remain under lower-level weather advisories.
