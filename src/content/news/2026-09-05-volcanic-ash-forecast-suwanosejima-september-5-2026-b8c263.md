The Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA) has released a volcanic ash forecast for Suwanosejima in Kagoshima Prefecture for September 5, 2026. The island is currently under Volcanic Alert Level 2 (Crater Perimeter Restriction).

## What travelers need to know

If an eruption occurs, ashfall and the fall of small volcanic rocks (up to 1cm in size) are forecast to affect areas of Toshima Village. The JMA has provided the following forecast windows for potential ash and rock dispersal:

* **00:00 – 03:00:** North-west direction; ash up to 80km, rocks up to 5km.
* **03:00 – 06:00:** North-west direction; ash up to 80km, rocks up to 4km.
* **06:00 – 09:00:** North-west direction; ash up to 60km, rocks up to 4km.
* **09:00 – 12:00:** North-west direction; ash up to 80km, rocks up to 4km.
* **12:00 – 15:00:** North-west direction; ash up to 70km, rocks up to 4km.
* **15:00 – 18:00:** West direction; ash up to 60km, rocks up to 4km.

These forecasts assume an eruption plume reaching 1,000 meters above the crater rim.

## What you should do

* If you are in the vicinity of Suwanosejima, monitor local municipal alerts closely.
* If you are within an area where small volcanic rocks are predicted to fall, move indoors or seek shelter under a sturdy roof immediately.
* Avoid entering restricted zones around the crater.

## What is confirmed so far

* The island remains at Volcanic Alert Level 2.
* The forecast is based on potential eruption activity occurring between 00:00 and 18:00 on September 5.
* Impact is specifically identified for Toshima Village, Kagoshima Prefecture.
