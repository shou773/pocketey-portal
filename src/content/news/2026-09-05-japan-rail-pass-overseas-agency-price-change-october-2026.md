Travelers planning to buy a **JAPAN RAIL PASS through an overseas JR-designated sales office or travel agency** should note a price increase from **October 1, 2026**.

The important detail is that the change is based on the **purchase date**, using local time at the place of purchase. The official online purchasing service keeps its current prices for a limited time, so the price you pay will depend on where and how you buy the pass.

## What travelers need to know

For overseas agency purchases made on or after October 1, the adult Ordinary Car prices change as follows:

| Pass | Through Sep. 30 | From Oct. 1 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 7 days | ¥50,000 | ¥53,000 |
| 14 days | ¥80,000 | ¥84,000 |
| 21 days | ¥100,000 | ¥105,000 |

Adult Green Car prices also increase:

| Pass | Through Sep. 30 | From Oct. 1 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 7 days | ¥70,000 | ¥74,000 |
| 14 days | ¥110,000 | ¥116,000 |
| 21 days | ¥140,000 | ¥147,000 |

Child prices rise as well. The official JAPAN RAIL PASS price page lists the full adult and child price table.

By contrast, the official online purchasing service currently keeps the existing prices: ¥50,000, ¥80,000 and ¥100,000 for adult Ordinary Car passes of 7, 14 and 21 days. The JR Group says this online price freeze is temporary and that it will announce when it ends.

## What you should do

- **Check how you plan to buy the pass.** The October change applies to purchases through overseas JR-designated agencies, not the official online purchasing service for now.
- **If using an overseas agency, pay attention to the purchase date.** Purchases made by September 30 use the current agency prices; purchases from October 1 use the revised prices.
- **Compare the official online option before paying.** For some travelers, the unchanged online price may be the better-value option after October 1.
- **Confirm the latest price before purchase.** JR has said the online price freeze is temporary, so this difference may not last indefinitely.

## Why this matters for trip planning

The increase is not huge on a single pass, but it can add up for couples, families or longer trips. A couple buying two adult 21-day Ordinary Car passes through an overseas agency after October 1 would pay ¥10,000 more in total than under the current agency price.

This is also a useful reminder that a JAPAN RAIL PASS is not automatically the cheapest option for every itinerary. Travelers should compare the pass price with the cost of the specific long-distance journeys they expect to make.
