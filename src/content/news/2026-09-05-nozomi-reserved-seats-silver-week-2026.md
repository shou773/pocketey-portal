Travelers using the Tokaido or Sanyo Shinkansen during Japan's September holiday period should plan ahead: **all Nozomi trains will operate with reserved seats only from September 18 through September 23, 2026**.

The rule applies to every Nozomi running between Tokyo and Hakata. During this period, the cars that normally provide non-reserved seating are converted to reserved seating.

## What travelers need to know

Nozomi is the fastest and most frequent service used on many popular routes linking Tokyo, Nagoya, Kyoto, Shin-Osaka, Hiroshima and Hakata.

During the September 18–23 peak period:

- **Nozomi has no non-reserved seats.** If you want to sit, you should reserve a seat in advance.
- **Hikari, Kodama, Mizuho, Sakura and Tsubame keep non-reserved seating as usual.** However, those trains may also be crowded during the holiday period.
- JR Central says the change is intended to reduce congestion and help more passengers secure seats before travel.

If you hold a non-reserved limited express ticket, JR Central says you may board a Nozomi and stand on the deck of an Ordinary Car, but you cannot take a seat without a reserved-seat ticket. Heavy crowding may also mean you cannot board the train you want, so relying on this option is risky.

## What you should do

- **Reserve as soon as your travel date is firm.** Standard reserved-seat tickets go on sale from 10:00 a.m. one month before departure.
- **Use an official online reservation service if convenient.** JR Central says some seats can be booked through Shinkansen Online Reservation up to one year in advance, subject to product and seat-availability conditions.
- **Build in flexibility.** If your preferred Nozomi is full, consider another departure time or travel date.
- **Do not assume Hikari or Kodama will be quiet.** Those trains retain non-reserved cars, but JR warns that non-reserved seating can still be crowded during peak travel periods.

## A note for JAPAN RAIL PASS users

A standard JAPAN RAIL PASS does not by itself cover travel on Nozomi. Pass holders need the separate **[ONLY WITH JAPAN RAIL PASS] NOZOMI MIZUHO Ticket** for a Nozomi journey.

During the all-reserved-seat period, reserving a seat is especially important. If a pass holder buys the non-reserved version of the additional Nozomi ticket, JR Central states that the traveler should stand rather than occupy a reserved seat.

## What is confirmed

- The reserved-seat-only period is **September 18–23, 2026**.
- It applies to **all Nozomi trains operating between Tokyo and Hakata** on the Tokaido and Sanyo Shinkansen.
- Other Shinkansen services listed by JR Central continue to offer non-reserved seating during the period.
