The Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA) has issued formal heavy rain warnings for multiple areas within Chiba Prefecture. These warnings include alerts for potential landslides and flooding in low-lying areas.

## What travelers need to know
As of September 7, 2026, the JMA has issued heavy rain warnings across various regions of Chiba, including the Chiba Central, North-Eastern, and Southern areas. Specific municipalities, such as Chiba City, Funabashi, and others, are under active warnings for either landslide risks or flooding. 

Additionally, the JMA has issued lightning, strong wind, and wave advisories for the prefecture. Due to previous infrastructure damage from earlier weather events, the JMA is applying provisional, lower-threshold criteria for issuing warnings in Chiba City and Yachiyo City.

## What you should do
- **Monitor local updates:** Check the [JMA website](https://www.jma.go.jp/) or local municipal disaster prevention portals for the most current information regarding your specific location.
- **Exercise caution:** Avoid mountainous areas, riverbanks, and low-lying regions prone to flooding. Follow all instructions provided by local authorities or hotel staff.
- **Check transport:** If you have travel plans, check the websites of train operators and bus companies for potential delays or service suspensions before heading out.

## What is confirmed so far
- The JMA has confirmed the issuance of heavy rain warnings (specifically for landslide and flooding risks) for various municipalities in Chiba Prefecture.
- A Level 4 heavy rain danger warning is currently in effect for Chiba City.
- Provisional, lower-threshold criteria for warnings are in effect for Chiba City and Yachiyo City.
