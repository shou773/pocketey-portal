# Pocketey Japan — Editorial & Monetization Principles

## Mission
Pocketey Japan exists to help international travelers understand what is changing in Japan and what they actually need to know before and during a trip.

Editorial decisions are traveler-first. Revenue is a consequence of usefulness and trust, not the reason an article exists.

## Editorial priority
When deciding whether to cover a topic, use this order:

1. Traveler usefulness
2. Real-world impact on a trip
3. Reliability of the information and availability of primary/official sources
4. Freshness or urgency
5. Long-term search and guide value
6. Natural monetization potential

A suggested 100-point candidate score is:
- Traveler Value: 35
- Trip Impact: 20
- Source Reliability: 20
- Freshness / Urgency: 10
- SEO / Evergreen Potential: 10
- Natural Monetization Fit: 5

Commercial potential must never override traveler value or source reliability.

## What Pocketey should cover
Prioritize practical changes and information that can affect an international visitor's decisions, plans, cost, safety, convenience, or experience in Japan, including:
- rail, buses, airports and local transport
- IC cards, tickets, fares and reservations
- attraction and facility changes
- seasonal events when they materially help trip planning
- weather and transport disruptions
- earthquakes and other disruptions when relevant to travelers
- tourism taxes and fees
- overtourism measures and local visitor rules
- immigration, airport and entry procedures
- luggage, batteries and travel rules
- openings, closures and route changes
- practical destination guides that answer a clear traveler need

Avoid publishing generic AI travel filler simply to increase article count.

## Article standard
A news/update article should make it easy to answer:
- What happened or changed?
- When does it take effect?
- Who is affected?
- Where does it apply?
- What should a traveler do differently?
- What is confirmed versus uncertain?
- Where is the official/primary source?

Prefer primary and official sources. Important claims should be verified before publication. AI-generated drafts remain drafts until human review.

## Editorial image policy
Pocketey should prefer real photographs over generated imagery, but image licensing and accuracy come before aesthetics.

Automatic image selection may use only images whose reuse terms can be identified and recorded. The first automated source is Wikimedia Commons, restricted to Public Domain, CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA material. Images carrying NonCommercial, NoDerivatives, non-free, unclear, personality-rights or trademark restrictions are not automatically accepted.

Every automatically attached image must retain:
- the image source page
- creator/credit information when available
- license name and license link when available
- alt text
- whether the image is illustrative or event-specific

For weather, disasters and other safety-sensitive updates, a generic location photograph must never imply that it depicts the current event. Such images are labeled as illustrative.

If no photograph passes the automated license/relevance filter, the draft is flagged for an AI-image fallback rather than silently using an uncertain web image. AI fallback imagery should be natural-looking, clearly reviewed by a human before publication, and must not fabricate documentary evidence of a real event.

Image selection remains part of the human publication gate. The editor should open the image source, check attribution/license requirements, and confirm that the image is not misleading before publishing.

## Content model
Use a NEWS → GUIDE → MONEY model without forcing every article through every stage.

NEWS earns attention and trust through timely useful updates.
GUIDE turns recurring traveler questions into durable resources and connects related information.
MONEY provides relevant commercial options only where they genuinely help the traveler complete the task.

## Related content
Add a restrained “You may also want to read” / related-reading module in the near future.

Recommendations should answer the question: “What is this reader genuinely likely to need next?” rather than merely matching a category. Usually show 2–4 highly relevant articles.

Internal links should help readers move naturally from a news update to a practical guide and, where appropriate, to planning resources.

## Affiliate policy
Affiliate links are allowed when they are contextually useful. Examples include accommodation, eSIM/connectivity, activities, airport transfers, transport-related services, luggage services and other legitimate travel-planning products.

Rules:
- Never create or distort an article primarily to sell an affiliate product.
- Do not insert an affiliate offer when it is unrelated to the reader's task.
- Prefer small editorial-style modules such as “Useful for your trip” or “Plan this trip.”
- Clearly disclose affiliate relationships.
- Editorial conclusions must not change because a service pays commission.

## Advertising policy
Pocketey may use limited page advertising and, later, direct sponsorships.

Advertising should feel compatible with Pocketey's calm editorial design. Avoid clutter, intrusive pop-ups, autoplay, deceptive UI, excessive ad density and aggressive calls to action.

Prefer a small number of clearly labeled placements. A custom-designed partner banner can be preferable to visually noisy generic display ads. Sponsored placements must be identifiable as advertising/sponsored content where appropriate.

## Reader experience hierarchy
The intended order is:

Traveler First → Useful Article → Related Content → Contextual Affiliate → Optional Advertising

The reader should be able to obtain the core answer without clicking an affiliate link or advertisement.

## AI publishing rule
AI can monitor sources, discover candidates, score them, research them, propose related guides and monetization relevance, and create English drafts.

AI should not autonomously publish factual travel updates by default. New AI-created articles should enter the repository with `draft: true` and be published only after human review changes the publication state.

## Brand guardrail
Pocketey should not become an AI affiliate-content factory. If increasing output reduces usefulness, source quality, clarity or trust, reduce output rather than lower the editorial standard.
