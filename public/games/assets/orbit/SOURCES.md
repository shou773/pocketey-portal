# Orbit Ribbon lunar relay

`lunar-relay.glb` is an original Pocketey model authored in Blender 4.3.2 from primitives with no imported artwork, models or textures.

Reproducible source: `docs/games/evidence/orbit-depth/relay.blender.py`.
58,032 bytes; 732 triangles; three opaque meshes and three materials.
The existing Kenney assets retain their own source and license records under `../kenney/`.
