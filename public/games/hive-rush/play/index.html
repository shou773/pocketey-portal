About Hive Rush

Hive Rush is a free daily word hive puzzle for your browser. Every day a fresh 7-letter honeycomb opens up: build as many four-letter-and-up words as you can, every word must use the gold center letter, and a single word that uses all 7 letters earns a +7 pangram bonus.

If you love NYT’s Spelling Bee but want unlimited plays without an account, this is your hive. Three modes are baked in: Daily for the shared puzzle of the day with shareable rank cards, Sprint 60s for a fast score-attack, and Endless for as many random hives as you want.

How to play

  • Tap or type any letter from the hive to build a word.
  • Every word must include the gold center letter.
  • Words must be 4 letters or longer; letters can repeat.
  • Pangrams (all 7 letters in one word) earn +7 bonus points.
  • Scores feed a 10-tier rank ladder from Beginner up to Queen Bee.

Why daily play matters

Hive Rush’s daily hive is seeded so every player gets the same 7 letters on the same day. Share your rank with the emoji grid card and chase the streak — coming back tomorrow keeps your fire alive. Sprint mode gives you a 60-second burst whenever you want a quick score chase, and Endless never runs out: a brand-new hive every tap.

Built mobile-first for portrait phones, installable as a PWA, no downloads needed.
